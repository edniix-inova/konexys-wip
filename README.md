# Konexys: Geospatial Intelligence for Connected Environments

**Konexys** is a knowledge-graph–enabled analytics platform designed to structure, contextualize, and extract insight from complex connected systems. 

While modern environments are saturated with connected devices, the data they produce often remains siloed. Konexys provides the architectural "connective tissue" to turn raw geolocated data into explainable system intelligence.

---

## 👁️ The Vision: Contextual Intelligence

The core philosophy of Konexys is that **location is the ultimate join-key**. By mapping connected devices onto a structured geospatial knowledge graph, the platform moves beyond simple data collection toward **system coordination**.

The framework is designed to be domain-agnostic. Whether managing a fleet of vehicles, a network of industrial sensors, or urban infrastructure, Konexys models the relationships between:
* **Physical Entities**: The devices and their specific geospatial constraints.
* **Logical Protocols**: How these devices interact and exchange data.
* **Operational Constraints**: The rules and goals of the specific environment.

---

## 🧠 Technical Architecture

### 1. The Knowledge Graph (Neo4j)
At the heart of the project is a graph database that formalizes the environment. This structure allows users to query complex relationships:
* **Spatial Topology**: Mapping junctions, edges, and geofenced zones.
* **Relational Context**: Understanding how a device's state affects its neighbors or the broader system.

### 2. Insight & Feature Engineering
Konexys enables users to engineer custom features based on their specific use cases. By traversing the graph, the platform supports:
* **Clustering**: Grouping devices by similarity of behavior or data output.
* **Path & Flow Analysis**: Understanding how entities move or how data propagates through the network.
* **Decision Support**: Moving from simple simulation to data-driven control.

## 🌿 Sustainability & Value Creation (Examples)

Konexys is built to support high-impact initiatives, particularly those aligned with **European Green Deal** objectives such as climate neutrality and reducing emissions. Potential applications include:

* **Clean Mobility**: Coordinating geofenced low-emission areas where connected devices adjust behavior based on precise location data.
* **Energy Optimization**: Managing power distribution or consumption in smart fleets to minimize carbon footprints.
* **Circular Economy**: Tracking the lifecycle and location of high-value assets to optimize reuse and resource efficiency.
* **Urban Intelligence**: Using geolocated data to reduce congestion and improve the quality of life in connected cities.
